## CUHK MSc E-Commerce and Logistics Technologies

The admissions procedure is published for **2027–28**. The current course structure explicitly says **2026–27**; it must not be labeled a confirmed 2027 syllabus.

The order/payment project relates to electronic payment integrity, e-commerce information exchange and supply-chain returns. Relevant modules include ECLT5720, ECLT5710, ECLT5940 and ECLT5970. It does not implement cryptography, production logistics optimization or distributed consensus.

Entry criteria normally require a recognized degree in engineering, business management, science or a related field with the stated honours/grade standard, or an accepted equivalent route. Do not convert a GPA into an eligibility guarantee. Project work does not replace grades or English requirements.

## CityUHK MSc FinTech and Digital Assets

**2027 entry** is published. The stablecoin case supports quantitative digital-asset exploration, with relevance to statistics and digital-asset topics; the payment case adds financial control/data-system foundations. Neither proves competence in blockchain development, security or regulatory compliance.

Recognized business-related degrees are among the normal backgrounds. Relevant professional experience is a preference, not a mandatory employment requirement. The personal statement is mandatory: one piece of at most 300 words in total, covering six specified questions in order, including own contribution and AI exposure. An English introduction video is optional, not a substitute. No GitHub requirement was established in the checked pages.
